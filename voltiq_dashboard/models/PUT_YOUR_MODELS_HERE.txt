Put your trained model files here (for example theft_classifier.pkl, forecast_model.pkl, anomaly_model.pkl).

1. Open ../model_hooks.py and fill in the functions you have a model for (the file explains how).
2. Optional: create models/model_info.csv to show your real metrics on the Model Info page.
   Columns: model_id,purpose,algorithm,version,trained_at,mae,rmse,precision,recall,f1,notes
   (purpose for the theft model must contain the word "Theft", e.g. "Theft classification")
